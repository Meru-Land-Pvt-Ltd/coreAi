# NODE 012 — ESCALATE

*The judgment to stop. 009 gave the machine a voice, 010 an ear, 011 a mind —
this gives it the one skill that makes an employee trustable: knowing when to
get the manager.*

## The dead end it kills

The day the library shipped, one question went unasked: what happens when the
library has nothing — and the customer is angry, or asking about money, or
about to leave? The machine said, honestly, "I don't have that information."
And then the mail died. The customer waited forever; the business never even
learned someone had asked.

No real employee works like that.

## What it does

When a run reaches Escalate, the whole thread lands in the **business's own
inbox**: who wrote, what they asked, what the agent already said, plus the
architect's standing note for the team. The mail's **Reply-To is the
customer** — the human answers the customer with one click of Reply. The
customer, meanwhile, reads one honest sentence: *"I'm passing this to the
team — they'll reply to you personally."* That sentence becomes the run's
text, so a Send email after it carries it without any wiring tricks.

## What it deliberately is NOT

Escalate does **not decide**. The Condition or Brain before it decides —
angry customer, refund, a question the library cannot answer — and one road
leads here. Judgment inside a Hand would be a hidden Brain, and the platform
forbids hidden Brains.

## The guards

- **One handover per run, ever.** A Loop cannot mail the owner twenty-five
  times; the second reach is refused politely, as a success.
- The handover mail counts against the same per-run email ceiling as every
  other mail — one cannon guard for the whole run.
- Every escalation is also recorded as an **unanswered question** — a growing
  list of what customers ask that the library cannot answer, waiting for the
  day the business reads it and uploads the missing page.

## The two sentences the architect owns

1. **What to tell the customer** — the honest sentence during handover.
2. **A note for the team** — standing guidance attached to every handover
   ("check the refunds sheet before promising anything").

Where handovers land is the **business's** — their Mail Setup at install
decides it, never typed by an architect.

## Why it sells

It is the answer to the one question every business asks before paying:
*"what if the AI gets it wrong?"* Answer: it knows when to stop, and you see
everything it stopped on — in your own inbox, ready to answer with one Reply.
