# NODE 013 — APPROVAL

*The probation. Autonomy is earned, visibly — never claimed.*

## The fear that stops every sale

The email employee was complete: it heard, knew, remembered, sorted, and
knew when to hand a human the thread. One thing still stood between it and a
real business switching it on: the owner's cold thought — *"what is it
telling MY customers when I'm not looking?"*

No business trusts a new employee unsupervised on day one. Human or machine.

## What it does

Approval is the sending hand **with permission attached**. The Brain drafts
the reply; Approval HOLDS it and mails the owner instead: the draft, the
architect's standing note, and a link with two buttons. **Approve** — the
held reply goes to the customer wearing the business's name, and the
customer's answer wakes the ear again. **Reply** — the owner answers
personally instead, because the approval mail's Reply-To is the customer.
One click either way, inside the tool every owner already uses: email.

Nothing reaches the customer without a yes. A draft nobody decides on
**expires honestly** — it is never sent late. And the page behind the link
obeys one subtle law: opening the link never decides (mail scanners prefetch
links — a prefetch that approved a mail would be the machine approving
itself); only the buttons decide, and deciding twice is answered honestly.

## The graduation is the product

For two weeks the owner approves drafts untouched. Then they swap Approval
for Send email — and that swap IS the moment the machine earned autonomy,
proven in the owner's own inbox, draft by draft. No AI platform wins trust
by claiming it; this one serves probation.

Approval and Escalate are siblings, not twins: **Escalate is the machine
saying "a human should handle this one"; Approval is the human saying "I
check everything until I trust you."** Between them, every fear a paying
business has is answered by mechanism, not promises.

## The law it proved

This node was built the same hour the founder asked the platform's hardest
question: *couldn't the AI Brain just BE escalation and approval?* The
answer became law: **prompts for thoughts, nodes for powers.** A Brain can
think "ask first" — it cannot hold a mail unsent. Approval holds exactly one
power and zero thoughts; the Condition before it does the thinking. That
division is what keeps a platform of thirteen nodes minimal.

## The guards

One approval request per run — a Loop never floods the owner. The request
counts against the run's shared mail ceiling. Where drafts land belongs to
the business's Mail Setup, never typed by an architect.
