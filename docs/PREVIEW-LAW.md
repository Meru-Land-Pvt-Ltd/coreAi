# THE PREVIEW LAW

Declared by the founder, 2026-08-25, after a week of a Preview that showed a
generic chat to an agent no customer will ever chat with.

**One sentence: the Preview is a mirror. It may show only what a real person
will see — and a mirror that shows anything else is broken.**

## Rule 1 — the canvas decides who is in the mirror, never a guess

- The agent **has Face nodes** → the mirror shows the **customer's page**,
  pixel-exact. Not one pixel more. (The founder's original pixel law.)
- The agent **has no Face and no way for a customer to speak to it** (Timer,
  webhook, manual only) → nobody ever types at it, and the person in front of
  the mirror is the **business**. The mirror shows their side: the one box
  they fill at setup, and a true sample of what will land in their inbox —
  drawn from a real run, never invented.
- A generic chat face for an agent that never chats is a face for a person who
  does not exist. It is the broken mirror this law was written against.

## Rule 2 — Run belongs where you are

Pressing Run shows the log where the architect is standing — a drawer on
Build, every step in order, in plain words. Run never throws anyone onto
another tab.

## Rule 3 — Advanced testing is dead

Nobody uses a console. Its two real organs moved where they belong: the run
log into Build's Run drawer, and the Test Email box beside the mail it sends.

## Rule 4 — nothing else ever enters the mirror

No face-switcher pills for agents with no face, no simulators, no debug
panels. If it is not what the customer or the business will see, it does not
enter.

## Who enforces it

The **AI Builder is the craftsman** of everything inside the mirror — every
pixel it composes answers to this law, the same way every node answers to the
SOP. The mirror's **frame** (tabs, drawers, empty states) obeys the law by
construction — it is the platform's own furniture, and no craftsman can be
blamed for it.
